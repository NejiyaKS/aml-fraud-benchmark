Two ways to break a fraud model

Decision-time evasion

The adversary perturbs a fraudulent transaction at inference time so the model misclassifies it as legitimate, while keeping the change financially plausible.

x_adv = x + δ  s.t.  f(x_adv) ≠ f(x),  ‖δ‖ₚ ≤ ε
Goal: fraud → legitimate Knowledge: white- to black-box

Training-data poisoning

The adversary injects corrupted samples into the training set, degrading the model — or planting a backdoor triggered by a specific pattern at inference.

D'_train = D_train ∪ D_p  →  f_θ'(x_t with T) = y_a
Goal: degrade or backdoor Vector: training pipeline

Empirical robustness findings

FINDING 01

MLP & SVM run more sensitive

Both models are the most exposed to gradient-based and margin-based perturbations — their smooth, high-dimensional decision boundaries are easier to nudge across.

FINDING 02

Random Forest is the most resilient

Ensemble averaging over many threshold-based trees makes it harder to find one perturbation that fools every tree at once — consistently the top performer under attack.

FINDING 03

Sanitization can be its own risk

The DBSCAN-based defense demonstrates that a poorly-tuned "cure" can be worse than the disease — accuracy loss from the defense itself, not the attack.

Production guidelines & latency trade-offs

Real-time inference

  • Feature squeezing / clipping — fast, low latency, effective against small perturbations.
  • Randomized smoothing — certified robustness, but adds meaningful latency; reserve for high-assurance paths.
  • Monitor live prediction distributions for sudden shifts.

Retraining & data integrity

  • Loss-based outlier detection — flag disproportionately high-loss samples offline.
  • K-NN consistency / Isolation Forest scrubbing — batch-only, too slow for real-time, but strong pre-training filters.
  • Version and lineage-track every model for fast rollback.

Latency trade-offs

  • High-throughput systems — favor simple defenses plus robust architectures like Random Forest.
  • High-assurance, low-throughput — afford heavier defenses like randomized smoothing.
  • Two-tier routing — fast first pass, async deep check on flagged transactions.
